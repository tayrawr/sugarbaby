# SugarBaby

A friction-free, human-friendly web application for logging, monitoring, and visualizing feline blood glucose levels, insulin therapy, feedings, and clinical observations.

## Language

**Pet**:
The feline patient receiving diabetes management and glycemic monitoring.
_Avoid_: Cat, animal, patient, profile.

**Reading**:
A blood glucose concentration measurement taken at a specific timestamp.
_Avoid_: Test, stick, poke, value, blood sugar.

**Dose**:
The quantity of insulin administered to the pet, measured in units.
_Avoid_: Shot, injection (used for the physical act, not the dose quantity).

**Feeding**:
An offering of food to the pet, recording the food item, portion offered, and percentage consumed.
_Avoid_: Meal, snack, portion.

**Food Preset**:
A saved template containing a food item name, default portion amount, and measurement unit for rapid entry.
_Avoid_: Recipe, favorite, preset meal.

**Appetite**:
The percentage (0% to 100%) of an offered feeding actually consumed by the pet.
_Avoid_: Consumption rate, intake, eaten amount.

**Cycle**:
A 12-hour therapeutic window initiated by an insulin dose (designated as AM Cycle or PM Cycle).
_Avoid_: Shift, period, block.

**Nadir**:
The lowest blood glucose reading recorded within a single 12-hour cycle, representing the peak action point of the insulin dose.
_Avoid_: Low point, bottom, trough.

**Target Range**:
The safe and therapeutic blood glucose window configured for the pet.
_Avoid_: Normal zone, safe zone, ideal range.

**Health Note**:
A qualitative observation recording pet behavior, symptoms, or body weight changes.
_Avoid_: Comment, memo, log entry.
